# Corrections

If you represent a company listed in `data/companies/` and a field about your
company is wrong or outdated, open a GitHub issue with:

1. The company and the field
2. What it should say
3. A public source: a filing, transcript, press release, or official page

Corrections with a source ship. That is the whole policy.

Two notes on scope:

- **Scores follow facts.** The quadrant and axis scores are the mechanical
  output of the rubric in [METHODOLOGY.md](METHODOLOGY.md) applied to the
  cited documents. If a fact changes, the score is recomputed from the
  anchors; scores are not negotiated separately from the facts under them.
- **This is a frozen snapshot.** The dataset is archived as of July 2026 and
  not actively maintained, so a correction updates the historical record
  rather than tracking your company going forward. The date stamp on every
  field is part of the claim.

Nothing here is investment advice, and no listed company or vendor has paid
to appear, to rank differently, or to change anything.
