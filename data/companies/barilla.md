## Rachel's notes

World's largest pasta maker. Barilla, Mulino Bianco, Pavesi, Wasa, Harrys, Pan di Stelle, Voiello. Private Italian family group. FY2024: €4.9B revenue (+0.3%), EBITDA €537M (+8%).

- Silent Builder. o9 + Databricks customer stories are the strongest public artefacts. No dedicated AI investor narrative; supply-chain-led tech org under CSCO Gianluigi Toia.
- BITE Innovation Centre (opened Nov 2025, Parma): 14,000 sqm, €20M+ investment. AI-driven smart sensors and an electronic nose for pasta drying optimisation. First named manufacturing AI use case.
- GenAI not explicitly mentioned in public Barilla communications. AI work is ML/sensor/process-driven, not LLM-based.
- No Microsoft, Google, AWS, Anthropic, or OpenAI public footprint. o9 and Databricks are the disclosed data and planning anchors.
- Watch FY2025 report (mid-2026) for any new AI disclosure.
