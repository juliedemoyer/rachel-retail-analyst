## Rachel's notes

L'Oréal is the rhetoric+production champion in the watchlist. Hieronimus mentions AI on every call, ModiFace acquisition (2018) was prescient, Beauty Genius is the GenAI flagship.

- Watch for partnership news with Nvidia / agentic-AI roadmap announcements.
- L'Oréal Beauty Tech team in Paris is ~600 strong.

### AGM 24 April 2026: dedicated AI presentation

At the Annual General Meeting on 24 April 2026, **Samuel du Retail**, General Manager AI – Data – Shared Services, delivered a stand-alone presentation on "the acceleration of Artificial Intelligence" at L'Oréal. Significance: AI is now a board-narrated, named-executive topic at L'Oréal, not just a CTO sub-theme. Stéphane Lannuzel (CTO) remains the AI presenter on quarterly earnings calls; du Retail owns the AGM-level AI narrative for shareholders. This positions L'Oréal alongside Kering (which appointed Pierre Houlès as Chief Digital, AI & IT Officer to ExCom on 17 March 2026) as the second EMEA luxury/beauty group with a dedicated, named AI seat at the top. LVMH still does not have one. Source: https://www.loreal-finance.com/eng/press-release/annual-general-meeting-24-april-2026

**Start date for du Retail in role**: not formally announced as a "promotion" press release; he has been in the GM AI – Data – Shared Services scope since at least early 2025 per CDO Magazine and Consumer Goods Technology coverage. The 24 Apr 2026 AGM keynote was the first time he was put in front of shareholders explicitly to narrate AI strategy. Sub-org note: **Lucas Fourès** appointed Chief Data and Analytics Officer at L'Oréal Luxe (the prestige division) in 2025 — separate role to du Retail's group-level scope.

### Anthropic case study — Claude for coding, analytics & SQL (April 2026)

L'Oréal featured in Anthropic's "Enterprise AI Transformation Guide for Retail" as a named customer using **Claude for employee-facing coding, analytics and SQL workflows**. Significance:

- **Adds Anthropic to L'Oréal's confirmed vendor stack** alongside Nvidia, Google Cloud and Microsoft. Multi-LLM is now explicit.
- **Use case is internal productivity**, not consumer-facing — complements Beauty Genius (consumer GenAI) and ModiFace (AR try-on). Closes the loop on the "marketing creative + consumer interface + employee analytics" stack.
- **Reads against** Carrefour's ChatGPT-shopping (consumer, OpenAI), Unilever's Brand DNAi (marketing governance), Zalando's Fashion Assistant (consumer, OpenAI). L'Oréal is the only luxury/beauty group on the watchlist with named Anthropic + employee-analytics deployment.
- **Production metrics (from a vendor case study, April 2026)**: 44K monthly users, 15K daily active users, 2.5M messages/month. Useful as a public benchmark for what "at scale" means for a conversational retail assistant.
- **GTM angle**: if you sell Anthropic into beauty/luxury, L'Oréal is the live reference. If you sell competing employee-AI (Microsoft Copilot, Glean, Hebbia), the bar is now Claude for SQL/analytics at those engagement levels.
