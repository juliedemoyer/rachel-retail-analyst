## Rachel's notes

Skincare R&D is the core moat: NIVEA, Eucerin, La Prairie, Aquaphor, Hansaplast. FY2025: €9.9B revenue (+2.4% organic). Q1 2026: group organic -4.6%, NIVEA -7% (rebalancing), Derma +8.2%, Eucerin China +87%.

- OpenAI confirmed: internal ChatGPT deployed late 2023 (within a year of public release) for analytics, formula development, and toxicology. Internal chatbot infrastructure existed since 2019. Disclosed in Hamburg Business interview with Dirk Ploss.
- Google Cloud confirmed as GenAI partner: public stage with Artefact on launching and scaling GenAI across Beiersdorf IT (Pierre Kröning + Stephan Abend).
- Artefact confirmed as consulting partner for GenAI programme.
- R&D AI footprint is deeper than consumer GenAI: Uncountable (formulation), Insilico (skin simulation), Beiersdorf Connect (startup ecosystem).
- Pierre Kröning (CIO since Sep 2025) also carries the Director Data, Analytics, AI + Automation title. Stephan Abend heads the AI + Automation function.
- Watch H1 2026 (early August) for any named AI tool or GenAI ROI disclosure.
