## Rachel's notes

Public Microsoft strategic partnership announced 2023, deepened 2024. Flagship UK retail AI reference. Sparks programme is central data asset.

### 2026-05-20 — FY2026 full year results

Revenue £17.3bn (+25%), adjusted PBT £671.4M (-23.8% vs prior year; H2 +4.1% confirming cyber recovery). Cyber incident cost £131.3M in adjusting items, offset by £100M insurance proceeds. "Year of two halves": significant H1 disruption followed by return to growth in H2.

AI confirmed in production: pricing optimisation, waste reduction, personalised customer offers. Sparks relaunched with wallet-based rewards as personalisation foundation. Digital and tech investment restarted H2: fashion planning platform, food warehouse management, e-commerce improvements.

Outlook: profit growth to resume vs FY24/25. CapEx £650-750M in FY26/27, ~two-thirds targeting Food.
