## Rachel's notes

Published Microsoft reference customer. Multi-year cloud migration largely on Azure. Competitor to AB InBev. EverGreen strategy is data-led.

### Leadership change: new CDTO (May 2026)

**Romain Apert** appointed Chief Digital & Technology Officer effective **15 May 2026**, replacing **Ronald den Elzen**. Announced via the official Heineken newsroom press release. CEO Dolf van den Brink framed the appointment around "scaling value from data and AI" — explicit AI mention at the C-suite hire level, which is a stronger signal than the previous role formulation.

What it means for Rachel's read on Heineken:

- AI is now a named priority of the CDTO mandate, not just a sub-theme of digital transformation. Watch for the first earnings call after 15 May where Apert (or the CEO referencing Apert's mandate) names a production AI deployment.
- The Microsoft anchor relationship is established (published reference customer, Azure-led EverGreen). Apert will inherit it; vendor stability is the base case until proven otherwise.
- Direct compare with AB InBev's BEES platform leadership. The EMEA beverage trio's AI and digital governance is evolving simultaneously.
