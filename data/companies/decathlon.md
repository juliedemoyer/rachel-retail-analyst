## Rachel's notes

Private company. 101K employees, 1,700+ stores. Strong innovation and sustainability culture. **Quadrant moved Narrative-led → Performer in May 2026** on the AI Factory, Conseilsport, and Artefact partnership signals, all cited below. Real production AI footprint in industrial / supply-chain / customer advice; consumer-facing surfaces growing.

### Leadership: Chief Data & AI Officer (since July 2025)

**Didier Mamma** promoted to Chief Data & AI Officer of Decathlon in **July 2025**, replacing Sébastien Hidocq. Previously VP Advanced Analytics, AI Innovation & Business Transformation at Decathlon since 2020. Background: SAP, Amadeus, Pivotal, Pitney Bowes; HEC MBA. Also serves as VP Data Value Creation for Decathlon United (the broader corporate structure). Source: https://www.republik-it.fr/decideurs-it/gouvernance/didier-mamma-promu-chief-data-ai-officer-de-decathlon.html

### Production AI footprint

- **AI Factory** — internal industrial-AI platform. Use cases include demand forecasting, supply-chain optimisation, store ops. Featured in Horrea (industrial-AI publication). Source: https://www.horrea.fr/en/resources/decathlon-ai-factory-data-into-performance/
- **Artefact partnership** — Decathlon presented at Artefact's "AI for Industry" Summit on industrial AI implementation. Source: https://www.artefact.com/blog/ai-for-industry-at-decathlon-ai-for-industry-summit-by-artefact/
- **Conseilsport AI assistant** — consumer-facing AI surface helping shoppers with sport / equipment advice on conseilsport.decathlon.fr. Source: https://conseilsport.decathlon.fr/sport-et-intelligence-artificielle-comment-lia-bouge-les-lignes
- **Decathlon Digital on Medium** — engineering team publishes regularly on AI work, including DevOps/ML pipelines. Recognised by Google Cloud DevOps Awards 2022. Sources: https://medium.com/decathlondigital/all?topic=artificial-intelligence and https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/devops-sre/devops-awards-2022-winner-decathlon-digital
- **Devoteam case study** — augmented-AI customer-experience implementation. Source: https://www.devoteam.com/fr/expert-view/retour-experience-decathlon-ia/

Direct peer compare: same sport-and-outdoor sector as Adidas (narrative-led) and On (silent builder). Decathlon is closer to On in the silent-builder mould — running real production AI without earnings-call narrative — but with broader sector reach (food, supply, store ops) than either.
