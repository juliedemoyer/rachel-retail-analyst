## Rachel's notes

Only UK-listed luxury house in the watchlist. Under turnaround (Joshua Schulman CEO, Burberry Forward strategy). H1 FY26 (Sep 2025): revenue £1.03B (-3%), adj. OP £19M (+1.8% margin). Q2 +2% comparable: first quarterly growth in 2 years.

- Penguin is the standout: internally-built transformer + fine-tuned LLM platform for luxury clientelling. +24% AVT, 100+ consultants across all markets. DataIQ Most Innovative Use of AI Global award. No hyperscaler named at the foundation-model layer.
- Databricks anchors the data layer (customer experience analytics + Snowplow). Same Databricks footprint as Barilla and select other luxury/CPG peers.
- Alloy Automation: staff sales workflow automation globally. Added Nov 2025.
- Charlotte Baldwin (CIO since Mar 2025): Costa Coffee/Coca-Cola heritage suggests data + omnichannel CRM emphasis.
- Giorgio Belloli (Chief Digital, Customer and Innovation Officer, 2023) carries the consumer digital narrative alongside Baldwin.
- Investor results URL: https://www.burberryplc.com/investors/results-and-reports (stable investor hub; individual PDF links change each reporting cycle).
