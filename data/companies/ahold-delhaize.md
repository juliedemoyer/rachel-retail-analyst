## Rachel's notes

Silent Builder graduating to Performer. Q1 2026 (reported 6 May 2026) confirmed the AI thesis with the first quantified ROI on watchlist grocery side. Two senior tech hires in 2025: Jan Brecht to Group CTO (first on ExCo), Ann Dozier to USA CIO. Eight named production tools now confirmed.

- Q1 2026: net sales €22.3B (+3.5% c/n, missed €22.59B consensus), underlying OP margin 4.0% (+0.2pt, beat consensus), diluted underlying EPS €0.62 (+8.9% c/FX, beat €0.613 consensus). Guidance reiterated: ~4% OP margin, mid-to-high-single-digit EPS growth.
- Albert Heijn has the deepest consumer GenAI stack in grocery: My AH Assistant + Scan & Kook + Steijn, all on Microsoft Azure OpenAI + EPAM.
- Bol Spot & Shop is the first consumer AI feature named in formal investor materials.
- U.S. side converging on Flybuy + Marty across all six chains.

### Q1 2026 AI takeaway — first quantified grocery AI ROI on watchlist

Two new AI metrics landed in Q1 2026 reporting and adjacent disclosures:

- **Albert Heijn bakery AI**: dynamic pricing + waste prediction system rolled out across NL stores. Outcome: **815,000 kg bread waste reduction in H2 2025**. This is the first named-tool-with-named-metric in EMEA grocery on the watchlist. Reads against Carrefour's ChatGPT-shopping (consumer adoption metric), L'Oréal's Claude deployment (employee productivity), Beauty Genius (scale-but-no-ROI). Bakery AI is the cleanest production ROI proof on the entire watchlist grocery side.
- **AH employee AI assistant**: 100,000+ Albert Heijn staff now using Azure OpenAI assistant for product, pricing, stock queries. Sits alongside L'Oréal's Claude (44K users, 99.9% analytics accuracy) as a credible employee-AI deployment number.

Together these elevate Ahold from "silent builder" to "production-quantified". Score upgraded: rhetoric 3 (unchanged), production 3→4. Watch Brecht's first earnings under his belt as CTO for whether he attaches more $/% metrics to the stack.

### Q1 2026 CEO succession — Thierry Garnier nominated

Disclosed alongside Q1 results (6 May 2026): **Thierry Garnier** nominated as next CEO; Frans Muller to retire in 2027. Garnier's pedigree is Kingfisher (CEO 2019–present, ran the AI/data transformation) + Carrefour (former CEO of Carrefour Asia, head of merchandising group). This is a Kingfisher-style operational pivot signal, with potential M&A and margin-reinvention thesis ahead. Note: Garnier is a known Microsoft/Azure customer at Kingfisher and worked closely with Pierre Bouchut (ex-CFO) on the digital transformation. Worth tracking whether Ahold's Azure footprint deepens or rebalances under his tenure.

### Leadership: Group CTO (effective 26 September 2025)

**Jan Brecht** appointed Chief Technology Officer + Executive Committee member, succeeding Ben Wishart. Brecht arrived from Nissan Motor (Chief Digital Information Officer); previously CIO at Mercedes-Benz Group and Adidas Group — the most senior cross-industry digital track on the watchlist. **Mike Laurenti** ended his CIO/CTO run at Ahold in April 2026; **Ann Dozier** appointed CIO Ahold Delhaize USA (regional, since 17 Feb 2025).
