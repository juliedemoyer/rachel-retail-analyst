§ 06 · About

Why this exists.

Rachel exists to answer one question honestly: across a whole sector, who is actually building with AI, with which vendors, at what maturity, and what did they really commit to on the last earnings call. Not the headline. The sentence in the transcript.

Keeping a finger on that industry AI pulse matters, but the alternative is hours on investor relations websites and analyst reports. That does not scale. An agent that reads every filing the night it publishes does.

So Rachel reads the investor reports, tracks the vendor relationships, and structures the result so every claim carries a source. When there is no source, she says so and stops. That refusal is the part that makes the rest usable.

The whole thing is open source under an MIT licence. Point it at your own market, write your own scoring anchors, run your own instance. Nobody should have to build their own Rachel from scratch.

§ 07 · Questions

FAQ

How are vendor scores produced?
Each score is derived from published customer stories, earnings call transcripts, press releases, and public CEO/CDO/CMO/CIO statements. Every company is mapped on two axes: board AI rhetoric warmth (1–5, from silent to AI-first narrative) and confirmed production use cases (0–5+, from no shipped output to 5+ named tools with quantified ROI). Where evidence is weak, the score is marked estimated rather than confirmed. Source URLs are attached at the vendor level so you can verify at any time.
How fresh is the data?
A running instance updates on earnings day: when a tracked company reports, the nightly ingestion reads the filing and refreshes that profile. The public repository ships a frozen July 2026 snapshot of that process — every score is stamped with the filing it derives from, and should be read as of that date.
Can I download the full dataset?
Yes. You can export the full vendor matrix as CSV, connect via API read access, or pipe a live data feed straight into your own tools and agents. That's where the magic happens.
Is this affiliated with Microsoft, Google, or Anthropic?
No. Rachel Retail is independently produced. No vendor pays to appear, rank better, or change their scores. The independence notice is in the footer.
Why EMEA to start?
Defensible beachhead, and a genuinely underserved market. North American retail is well-covered by US-focused products, partly because the SEC's EDGAR platform centralises every US filing in one place, making it relatively straightforward to run automated analysis at scale. In EMEA, there is no equivalent. Every company publishes its annual reports, earnings presentations, and transcripts on its own website, in its own format, on its own schedule. Tracking the top EMEA retailers and consumer brands means building and maintaining 50+ individual data pipelines. That's the moat. Expanding to 300+ globally is the roadmap.
I'm an equity analyst, what does Rachel Retail do for me specifically?
Replace four hours of Euromonitor with a four-minute search. Rachel Retail gives you AI CapEx exposure scores (0–5) per stock, vendor footprint breakdowns, and the exact earnings language CEOs and CFOs used to frame AI investment: all cited, all structured, all comparable across the sector. CSV export of the full vendor matrix is available for modelling.
How is this different from HG Insights?
HG Insights tells you what tech is installed: a lagging indicator of past buying decisions. We tell you what AI strategy the CEO and CTO committed to on the last earnings call: a leading indicator of what they'll buy next. Two different data types, two different moments in the sales cycle. HG also covers all sectors and all tech globally; we go deep on consumer & retail, with financial context, earnings calendar, and source citations that HG doesn't provide.
